# Portfolio

Language for the portfolio's blog, discussions, and newsletter.

## Language

**Comment**: A reader's contribution to a post's discussion, either at the top level or as a reply. A deleted comment remains as a placeholder and retains its replies.

**Reply**: A comment attached to a parent comment in the same discussion.

**Comment count**: The number of active comments on a post, including replies and excluding deleted entries.

**Pending subscription**: A newsletter signup awaiting confirmation from its email recipient. Repeat signup requests resend confirmation without invalidating earlier confirmation links.

**Confirmed subscription**: A newsletter signup whose recipient has confirmed their email address and has not unsubscribed. A welcome email is a notification, not a condition of confirmation.

**Unsubscribed subscription**: A newsletter signup withdrawn by its recipient. Signing up again requires fresh confirmation.

**Table of contents**: A post's heading navigation, in reading order. Each entry uses the corresponding displayed heading's text, identity, and depth.
